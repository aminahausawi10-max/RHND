Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Renewed Hope Nigeria Diaspora services

How do I register as a diaspora member?
Click on the Register button in the top navigation. Complete the simple registration form with your full name, email, current host country, and create a password. You will instantly receive access to your Member Dashboard and a unique Diaspora ID number.
How do I apply for passport renewal assistance?
Log in to your Member Dashboard, go to Diaspora Services, and select "Passport Renewal Assistance". This will open a pre-filled support ticket for our admin team to process your request.
What is the Diaspora Membership ID Card?
Every registered member receives a Digital Diaspora Membership ID with a unique number (e.g. RHND-NIG-00001). This official credential can be used for accessing diaspora services, consular support, and priority government assistance programs.
How do I contact the Nigerian Embassy or Consulate?
Visit the Diaspora Services page for a directory of embassies and consulates worldwide. You can also submit a ticket through your dashboard and our team will connect you with the appropriate consular office.
Is this platform affiliated with the Federal Government of Nigeria?
The Renewed Hope Nigeria Diaspora (RHND) platform is a community-driven information hub that aggregates verified government policies and resources. It is designed to support and empower Nigerians living abroad.
I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
Currently, please contact our support team via email at support@rhnd.gov.ng or call the Diaspora Helpline to request a password reset. A self-service password reset feature will be available in a future update.

Still Need Help?

Our dedicated diaspora support team is available to assist you with any questions or concerns.
