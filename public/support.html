Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Renewed Hope Nigeria Diaspora services

How do I register as a diaspora member?
Click on the Register button in the navigation. Complete the simple registration form with your full name, email, current host country, and create a password. You will instantly receive access to your Member Dashboard and a unique Diaspora ID number.
How do I apply for passport renewal assistance?
Select "Passport Renewal Assistance" in the Support Ticket form above or log in to your Member Dashboard. Provide your current passport number and preferred consular center. Our support desk will review and provide priority appointment guidance.
What is the Diaspora Membership ID Card?
Every registered member receives a Digital Diaspora Membership ID with a unique number (e.g. RHND-NIG-00001). This official credential can be used for accessing diaspora services, consular support, and priority government assistance programs.
How do I contact the Nigerian Embassy or Consulate?
Visit the Diaspora Services page for a directory of embassies and consulates worldwide. You can also submit a ticket through this page and our team will connect you with the appropriate consular office.
Is this platform affiliated with the Federal Government of Nigeria?
The Renewed Hope Nigeria Diaspora (RHND) platform is a dedicated community information hub that aggregates verified government policies, diplomatic resources, and citizen services to support Nigerians living abroad.
